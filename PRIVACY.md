# Radiophos privacy note

*Last updated: 14 November 2026*

**Radiophos collects no personal data.** There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no
tracking. The developer receives nothing from the app.

## What stays on your device

Your favorites, playlists, saved songs, settings, scheduled recordings and recordings are
stored only on your device. Recordings contain a tag naming the station and the time;
nothing about you.

## Who the app talks to

To work, Radiophos connects directly to:

- **Radio Browser** (radio-browser.info), the community station directory: to list and
  search stations. Their servers see your IP address and what you search for.
- **The radio stations** you play or record, and the servers hosting their logos: they see
  your IP address, like any internet radio player.
- **Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music**, only when you tap "Find" on a saved song: the
  song's title opens in that service.
- **A station's own website**, only when you tap "Web" on it.

These services have their own privacy policies.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (iPhone, optional): reminders about scheduled recordings.
- **No location access.** The Orbit view places "you are here" from your device's time zone,
  worked out on the device.
